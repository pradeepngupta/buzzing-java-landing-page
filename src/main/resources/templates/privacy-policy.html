PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information.

This policy explains how Buzzing Java handles information submitted through the book launch waitlist.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Information we collect

When you join the Buzzing Java book launch waitlist, we collect the name and email address you provide so we can identify your submission and contact you about the project. The form also asks for a launch-party preference. You can optionally select topics you are interested in and provide another interest in your own words. If you arrive through a campaign link, campaign parameters associated with that link may also be recorded to help us understand how people find the site. Please do not submit sensitive personal information in optional fields.

Why we use it

We use waitlist details to organize signups, send updates about the book and its launch, and share relevant previews or event invitations. Optional interest selections help us understand which subjects readers want to hear about. We also use website analytics to understand site activity in aggregate and improve the visitor experience. Information submitted through the waitlist is not displayed as public website content.

Service providers

To operate the site, waitlist, communications, and related workflows, information may be processed by service providers used for those purposes. Depending on the workflow in use, these providers include Kit and Brevo for email delivery and subscriber management; Google Sheets for waitlist storage; Umami and Ahrefs for website analytics; n8n for workflow automation; and Slack for internal notifications. The providers and the information handled can depend on which workflow is active. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

We keep waitlist information only while it is needed for the launch and communication purposes described here. The project has not announced a new launch date, so the timing of those communications may change as the project develops. We do not state a fixed retention period on this page. To ask us to delete your waitlist information, email pradeep@buzzingjava.com with the email address you used to join and the subject “Delete my Buzzing Java data.” We will pass the request through the relevant services used by the active workflow. You can also contact us to ask about correcting your information.

Your choices

Joining the waitlist is voluntary. You may unsubscribe from email updates at any time using the unsubscribe link in a message or by emailing the address above. If you unsubscribe, we will stop sending the updates covered by that subscription. Unsubscribing is separate from deleting information already held in the waitlist, so contact us directly if you also want your information deleted. You may ask us to correct information that is inaccurate. When contacting us, include enough detail for us to identify the relevant signup; do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Questions about this policy

This policy describes information associated with the Buzzing Java website and book launch waitlist. If you have a question about what you submitted, how it is used, or a request to update or remove it, email pradeep@buzzingjava.com. We may update this page if the site or the waitlist process changes. Please review the posted version for the current description of our practices.
